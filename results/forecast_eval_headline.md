## Headline (hand-written)

**Negative result: no wind forecaster raises the share of sections meeting C_req by a meaningful margin, in either loop.** Pooled changes are within +-0.5 pp; among the forecaster arms, the largest per-mismatch gain whose CI excludes 0 is +0.3 pp (`ar1`, fast loop, no mismatch, where the forecaster has no gust to track). The only larger one is `nominal+gust`, which does not forecast at all: +0.4 pp [+0.0, +0.7] at high mismatch in the fast loop, bought with +0.9 % [+0.7, +1.2] volume from a wider wind margin. 20 seeds x 4 presets x 4 mismatch levels, paired against forecaster-off on the same passes; pooled = low + med + high mismatch, all presets.

* **Forecast quality improves, modestly.** AR(1) cuts wind MAE by 0.059 m/s [-0.085, -0.033] in the fast loop (off: 0.558 m/s, about 11%) and by 0.099 m/s [-0.130, -0.068] in fast + slow (the forecaster keeps learning over 5 passes). EWMA: -0.042 [-0.082, -0.002] and -0.042 [-0.082, -0.002] (fast, fast + slow); `last` does not help (+0.002 [-0.040, +0.046]). The pilot's -0.18 m/s for AR(1) came from 3 seeds and did not hold. The best possible forecaster (`gust_oracle`, MAE 0) is 0.558 m/s better than off.
* **Success does not move.** Fast loop, change in segments meeting C_req vs off (off = 0.940, fair oracle = 0.958): `ar1` -0.0 pp [-0.3, +0.3], `ar1+gust` -0.0 pp [-0.4, +0.3], `ewma` -0.1 pp [-0.4, +0.2], `last` -0.5 pp [-0.9, -0.1]. Fast + slow (off = 0.960, fair oracle = 0.958): `ar1` -0.2 pp [-0.4, +0.1], `ar1+gust` -0.1 pp [-0.4, +0.2], `ewma` -0.1 pp [-0.3, +0.2], `last` -0.3 pp [-0.6, -0.1]. The CIs exclude gains of more than about 0.4 pp for every deployable arm.
* **Volume is a wash or slightly worse.** `ar1`: +0.0% [-0.2, +0.2] (fast), -0.1% [-0.3, +0.1] (fast + slow). The gust-aware arms buy nothing for their volume: `ar1+gust` +0.6% [+0.4, +0.7] (fast), +0.4% [+0.2, +0.6] (fast + slow) at -0.0 pp [-0.4, +0.3] and -0.1 pp [-0.4, +0.2] met. `nominal+gust` (no forecast, only a wider wind margin) does the same: +0.6% volume, +0.1 pp [-0.1, +0.4] fast, -0.0 pp [-0.2, +0.2] fast + slow. So gust-aware planning is a volume cost, not a forecast benefit.
* **The upper bound explains it.** `gust_oracle` (exact wind of the next section, not deployable) does not help either: -0.5 pp [-0.9, -0.1] met in the fast loop and -0.3 pp [-0.6, -0.0] in fast + slow, volume -0.5% [-0.7, -0.2] and -0.3% [-0.6, -0.1]. A perfect wind forecast cannot raise success here, so no real one can. The settings the selector picks are insensitive to wind: a 30% stronger wind (1 sd of gust at mismatch high) changes coverage by only -0.07 to -0.18 pp and drift by +0.8 to +2.6 x1e-3 (sensitivity table below). The selector's safety margin already absorbs this risk, and the remaining shortfall comes from effects the wind forecaster cannot see (canopy interception, nozzle wear, card noise). The small negative `gust_oracle` and `last` differences come with lower volume, consistent with the selector spraying less when it believes the wind is calm; they are not an error-reduction effect.
* **Fast loop leaves a gap to the fair oracle that wind does not explain.** Off meets C_req in 0.940 of sections vs 0.958 for the fair oracle (pooled), and `gust_oracle` closes none of it (0.935). Fast + slow already matches the fair oracle (0.960 vs 0.958), so there is no room left for any forecaster there.
* **Where something stands out (all small, one of many CIs, treat as chance unless repeated).** Fast, no mismatch: `ar1` +0.3 pp [+0.1, +0.5] (volume +0.1% [+0.0, +0.3]). Fast, high mismatch: `nominal+gust` +0.4 pp [+0.0, +0.7] at +0.9% [+0.7, +1.2] volume, `last` -0.9 pp [-1.8, -0.3]. Fast + slow, calm_morning: `ar1+gust` +0.5 pp [+0.1, +1.0] at +0.4% volume, but `nominal+gust` gives +0.2 pp [+0.1, +0.5] at +0.6% with no forecast. Fast + slow, med mismatch: `ar1` -0.3 pp [-0.6, -0.0]. No arm improves success in a way that exceeds its no-forecast control.
* **Recommendation.** Leave the forecaster off by default (it is). Report it as a tested extension with a negative result: it reduces forecast error but not coverage failures in this simulator. Caveat: simulator-internal, gust model is multiplicative AR(1) on speed only, so this is the best case for a persistence-based forecaster.
