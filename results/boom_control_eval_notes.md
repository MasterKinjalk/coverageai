## Verdict

Default stays off. Height control does not pay off with the nominal or learned selector model; it pays off only with the fair oracle, and only for drift.

- Open loop, height alone: drift rises, success unchanged. Nominal model: +0.0033 [+0.0010, +0.0057] drift, +0.0000 [-0.0200, +0.0200] success. Learned model: +0.0027 [+0.0005, +0.0049] drift, +0.0100 [-0.0150, +0.0350] success. The selector uses the height options to cut volume (24.83 to 21.42 GPA, nominal), not drift.
- Open loop, speed+height: drift falls, success does not clearly change. Nominal: -0.0029 [-0.0049, -0.0011] drift, -0.0200 [-0.0400, -0.0050] success. Learned: -0.0029 [-0.0042, -0.0015] drift, -0.0050 [-0.0250, +0.0150] success.
- Closed loop, fast loop, speed+height: lower drift and volume, fewer sections meeting the target. Fraction met -0.0287 [-0.0362, -0.0206] (med) and -0.0344 [-0.0444, -0.0244] (high); GPA -2.5927 [-3.0943, -2.1093] and -2.8117 [-3.3392, -2.3181]; drift -0.0021 [-0.0045, +0.0003] (med, CI includes 0) and -0.0035 [-0.0066, -0.0005] (high). Height alone also loses target: -0.0162 [-0.0244, -0.0087] (med), -0.0162 [-0.0262, -0.0062] (high).
- Closed loop, fair oracle, height alone: drift falls clearly with no detectable loss in success. Drift -0.0036 [-0.0052, -0.0019] (med) and -0.0060 [-0.0075, -0.0046] (high); fraction met -0.0050 [-0.0125, +0.0031] and -0.0019 [-0.0087, +0.0050]; GPA -1.4932 [-1.8344, -1.1782] and -1.2959 [-1.5151, -1.0848].
- Closed loop, fair oracle, speed+height: drift falls (-0.0032 [-0.0049, -0.0014] med, -0.0066 [-0.0082, -0.0051] high) but fraction met also falls (-0.0213 [-0.0313, -0.0113], -0.0269 [-0.0369, -0.0175]). Adding speed to height costs 2 to 3 pp even with the oracle.

Reading: the drift benefit of lower release height exists in the simulator (fair oracle). The nominal and learned models do not predict the height effect well enough for the selector to realize it, and the fast loop gives up 1.6 pp of sections meeting the target for it. Speed+height is net negative on the target in every closed-loop cell. This is a negative result for enabling height control by default. All closed-loop differences are on oracle_nominal with a perfect verifier, 4 presets x 10 seeds.
