# Learned card feedback v2: trained on calibrated cards (D29, D30)

Generated by `experiments/train_feedback_model.py --version 2`. Do not edit by hand. v1 is unchanged (`results/feedback_model.md`, `models/feedback_*_v1.joblib`). Closed-loop evaluation of v2 on fresh seeds: `results/feedback_model_v2_closed_loop.md`.

Everything here is simulation. The models learn the hidden mismatch of this simulator and its card noise. The reader calibration (D30) subtracts the reader's mean error per coverage band, taken from the reader's own validation on synthetic cards with known truth; a real reader would need annotated real cards, and a real feedback model real verified cards.

## What changed from v1

- The noisy reader is wrapped in the reader calibration (`calibrate_reader=True`); the perfect reader is unchanged. Same features (version 1), same seeds, same grid.
- 3 rounds instead of 2: round 1 open + rule; round r >= 2 fast with the round-(r-1) XGBoost model. The final models are fit on the union of all rounds.

## Data

- Training seeds 20000 to 20119 (120); validation 21000 to 21029 (val-A 21000-21019: early stopping and configuration; val-B 21020-21029: tables only).
- Round 1: 921,600 training rows, 230,400 validation rows, generation 789 s.
- Round 2: 460,800 training rows, 115,200 validation rows, generation 532 s.
- Round 3: 460,800 training rows, 115,200 validation rows, generation 532 s.
- Total 2,304,000 rows, generation 30.9 min on 26 workers.

## What was not tested

- Real cards, real readers and real fields. The calibration table and the hidden mismatch are the simulator's own.
- The image readers in the training data (rows come from the perfect and the calibrated noisy reader).
- A calibration table that is itself wrong (for example a reader whose bias drifts with lighting or card batch). The model was trained with the exact table the simulated reader follows.
- The on-policy check uses the validation seeds that also served for early stopping (val-A) and the tables (val-B); the closed-loop evaluation on fresh seeds is in the closed-loop write-up.
