# Physics-first hybrid vs pure ML vs pure physics

Generated by `experiments/eval_hybrid.py --stage report` from `results/hybrid_eval.json`. Model: `src/coverageai/model/hybrid.py` (`PhysicsResidualModel`).

## Design

* **Physics base**: the simulator's equations with no hidden mismatch and the mean WSP spread factor (identical to `oracle_nominal`); its own interval is the coverage at the 10 % / 90 % spread-factor quantiles.
* **Residual**: LightGBM, 300 trees, 15 leaves, min 200 rows per leaf, L2 = 10, learning rate 0.05 (strongly regularized), on the SprayModel features plus the physics outputs (coverage, its spread-factor band, retention, drift, displacement). Targets: coverage residual (pp), retention residual, drift residual, displacement residual.
* **Interval**: quantile LightGBM (q = 0.1 / 0.9) on the coverage residual, conformalized on a 15 % calibration split with a separate one-sided offset per bound (each bound targets 10 % miss).
* **Misspecified base** (`MISSPEC_PRESETS`), multiplicative errors on the true parameters: `optimistic`: evaporation_lambda_scale x1.3, drift_threshold_scale x0.75, spread_factor_scale x1.1, vmd_scale x0.85; `pessimistic`: evaporation_lambda_scale x0.75, drift_threshold_scale x1.25, spread_factor_scale x0.9, vmd_scale x1.2.
* **Slow loop**: `fit(records)` appends records and refits the residual (legacy refit mode); the closed-loop experiments wrap the model in `feedback.ResidualCorrectedModel`, like every other model.
