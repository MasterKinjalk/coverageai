## Negative results

1. **Prediction-error variance does not shrink pass after pass.** The slow loop removes the mean residual (high mismatch: -6.02 pp in pass 1, 0.03 pp in pass 2), but the within-pass residual variance stays at 27.0 to 31.9 pp^2 in every pass and at every mismatch level, including none. Per-card spread-factor noise and per-segment gusts are irreducible for an offset correction.
2. **B1 is less safe outside the generator than on our own cards.** MAE 0.20 pp and 99.4% class agreement on renderer cards, but 1.74 pp (bias -1.74 pp) and 88.3% on the Simoes synthetic cards; class agreement misses 90% for both readers. Real-card accuracy is unknown.
3. **The wind forecaster does not help.** AR(1) lowers the wind error (-0.059 m/s [-0.085, -0.033]) but not coverage failures (-0.0 pp [-0.3, +0.3]); even a perfect gust forecast gives -0.5 pp [-0.9, -0.1].
4. **Boom height (alone or with speed) trades success for drift and volume.** In the fast loop speed + height cuts volume (-2.81 [-3.34, -2.32] GPA, high) but meets C_req less often (-2.9 pp [-3.6, -2.1] med, -3.4 pp [-4.4, -2.4] high). Height alone raises drift in the open loop (+0.0027 [+0.0005, +0.0049], learned model). Speed alone changes success by +0.0 pp [-2.0, +2.0].
5. **The hybrid predictor is not better than pure ML in the closed loop**: -0.7 [-1.1, -0.3] pp met against `SprayModel` with `fast_slow`, and the exact-base (circular) hybrid is not better either.
6. **The midterm slow loop hurt.** Refitting LightGBM on the cards with record weight 30 gave -89% [-308, -16] recovery (met 0.832); it narrowed the interval while fitting card noise. Replaced by the residual offset (D16).
7. **The volume target is unreachable against the card-aware oracle.** The fair oracle itself uses +12.6% [+11.2, +13.9] more volume than `oracle_card`; fast + slow is at +17.2% [+15.4, +19.0].
8. **The fast loop alone does not clear the recovery target with confidence**: 69% [44, 82], weakest on `hot_dry` (55% [4, 73]).
9. **Rendered-card verifiers are inconclusive in the loop**: 5 seeds give 46% [-67, 78] (B0) and 46% [-100, 76] (B1) recovery. Re-treatment flags were never raised, so that path is untested.
