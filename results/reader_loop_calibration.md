# Card reader calibration measured on closed-loop cards (D30)

Generated by `experiments/calibrate_reader_loop.py`. Do not edit by hand. Everything is simulation: the cards are rendered by the simulator, and a real reader would need annotated real cards.

Why: the table measured on the reader's validation cards (`results/<reader>_synthetic.json`) reversed the classic reader's bias when used in the loop (`results/feedback_model_v2_closed_loop.md`). This table is measured on the cards the loop itself produces.

Protocol: development seeds 20000-20005 to fit and 20006-20007 held out; 6 presets x c_req 20 / 30 / 40 x mismatch med; policy fast with the eq. 3 rule and the raw reader; one nozzle per field. Bands on true coverage; bias = mean(reading - true). The held-out check applies each table to the stored raw readings, which is an approximation of what happens in the loop. Wall time 10.2 min.

## b1

Skipped: single-process one b1 pass cost 2.0x a b0 pass (30.5 s against 15.2 s), but run in parallel with 26 workers b1 passes were about 6x slower than b0 passes (36 b1 passes took about 1,060 s of wall time against about 170 s for 36 b0 passes), so the run was stopped after 36 of 144 b1 passes and b1 was dropped; its partial cards were not used.

## What was not tested

- Mismatch levels other than med, the free nozzle choice, other policies (the table was measured under the rule; a learned policy picks somewhat different settings).
- Real cards. The table corrects the reader for the simulator's renderer only.
- A table per droplet class or per nozzle; this one depends on coverage only.
