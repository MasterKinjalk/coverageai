# Learned card feedback: training and offline validation (D29)

Generated by `experiments/train_feedback_model.py`. Do not edit by hand. Closed-loop results are in `results/feedback_model_closed_loop.md`.

Everything here is simulation. The models learn the hidden mismatch of this simulator (canopy interception, nozzle wear, AR(1) gusts) and its card noise. They are not evidence about a real field. A real system would have to be trained on real verified cards.

## Data

- Training: scenario seeds 20000 to 20119 (120 seeds), 6 presets, mismatch none / low / med / high, nozzle mode one-per-field and free, readers perfect and noisy, target c_req drawn per cell from {20, 25, 30, 35, 40}.
- Round 1 (behaviour open and fast with the rule): 921,600 training rows (23,040 passes, 608 s).
- Round 2 (behaviour fast with the round-1 XGBoost feedback): 460,800 more rows (11,520 passes, 413 s).
- Validation: seeds 21000 to 21029, same grid and both rounds, 345,600 rows. Seeds 21000 to 21019 (val-A) were used for early stopping and the choice of configuration; seeds 21020 to 21029 (val-B) only for the tables below.
- Total generation time 21.6 min on 26 worker processes. One row per section (40 sections per pass); the target is the card error (card reading - raw model prediction).

## Offline accuracy on val-B (round-2 models)

Each correction is compared with the card error it is trained on and with the actual error (true coverage - raw model prediction), which is what matters for the field. The rule column is exactly the correction the eq. 3 rule would apply at that point of the pass. The pass-mean column is a diagnostic, not a policy that was run: the plain average of all card errors read so far in the pass (0 before the first card), the feature the models rely on most. Model corrections are clipped to +-15 pp, like the rule's. Behaviour policies: open, fast with the rule, fast with the round-1 XGBoost model.

## What was not tested

- Real cards and real fields. The hidden mismatch is the simulator's own, of a few known kinds; a model trained on it has learned those kinds and nothing else.
- The image readers (b0, b1) in the training data: rows come from the perfect and the noisy (image-free) readers only.
- Other targets than {20, 25, 30, 35, 40} %, other lambda values, speed and boom-height control, the wind forecaster, and the slow loop (fast_slow) with a learned fast loop.
- The round-2 validation rows were produced with the round-1 XGBoost model, not with the final one.
- val-A was used for early stopping and for the choice of configuration, so its numbers are optimistic; the tables use val-B only. The offline gain says nothing yet about the closed loop.
