For a course project (ABE 598) we are building a closed-loop precision-spraying simulation in which a water-sensitive paper (WSP) card image is analysed to measure the achieved coverage and feed it back to the next spray segment.

Your paper, "Spray Quality Assessment on Water-Sensitive Paper Comparing AI and Classical Computer Vision Methods" (Agriculture 2025, 15(3):261), and the open synthetic dataset on Zenodo (10.5281/zenodo.13995950) have been very useful. We are using the 300 synthetic cards to develop and test our segmentation and coverage-estimation code, and we will cite the paper and the CC BY 4.0 licence.

The latest Zenodo version (10.5281/zenodo.14010262) lists two manually annotated real WSP images (2,754 droplets) and 127 pre-annotated real squares, but its files are restricted. We would be grateful if you could grant us access, or share those files directly, so that we can validate our coverage estimates on real cards and not only on synthetic ones. 3. Any usage conditions beyond CC BY 4.0 that you would like us to observe.

The images would be used only for this course project (evaluation of a coverage-measurement module). We will credit the dataset and the paper in our report and are happy to share our results with you afterwards.
